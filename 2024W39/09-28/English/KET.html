KET Sample Exam

Part 1: Reading and Writing (1 hour)

Reading (30 minutes)

Instructions: Read the text and answer the questions.

Text Example:

Mark enjoys photography. He often takes his camera to the park to capture beautiful nature scenes. Last weekend, he took pictures of flowers and birds. Mark plans to create a photo album of his favorite shots.

Questions:

  1. What does Mark enjoy doing?
  2. Where does he often take his camera?
  3. What does he plan to create?

Writing (30 minutes)

Task: Write a short paragraph (about 30-50 words) about your favorite food. Include:

  • What it is
  • Why you like it
  • When you usually eat it

Part 2: Listening (30 minutes)

Instructions: Read the text below and answer the questions.

Text Example:

Conversation between two friends:

Anna: Hey, Tom! Are you coming to the concert on Saturday?

Tom: Yes, I am! I’m really looking forward to it.

Anna: Me too! Do you want to meet at my house first?

Tom: That sounds good! What time?

Questions:

  1. What event are Anna and Tom talking about?
  2. Where will they meet before the concert?
  3. How does Tom feel about the concert?

Part 3: Speaking (8-10 minutes)

Instructions: You will have a conversation with the examiner.

  1. Introduction: The examiner will ask you to introduce yourself.

  2. Questions: Answer questions about your daily routine, hobbies, and family.

    • What do you do in the morning?
    • What is your favorite hobby?
    • How many people are in your family?

  3. Role Play: The examiner will give you a situation, and you will respond accordingly.

Tips for Preparation:

  • Read different types of texts to strengthen reading skills.
  • Write about personal experiences to improve writing.
  • Listen to various English materials for better understanding.
  • Practice speaking on different topics to gain confidence.

Good luck with your preparation!
